Which template is used

For each event and channel StarSign uses your organisation's active template first, then the platform default (closest locale first), then its built-in wording. Customise a platform default to change the wording for your organisation only.

Delivery through Novu

StarSign renders the subject and body from these templates, then triggers one Novu workflow per event ({{ novuPrefix }}<event> with the default prefix). The Novu workflow must use the payload's subject and body: wording written in Novu is not used. When Novu is turned off, notifications are recorded as suppressed and nothing is sent.
